Bio;

Architect and eco-social designer with a background in furniture, interiors, and spatial research. I've always been drawn to the questions underneath the project — who a space is built for, what it carries, and what it leaves out.

I'm drawn to projects where design, territory, and community overlap.
Based between Ecuador and Italy.
